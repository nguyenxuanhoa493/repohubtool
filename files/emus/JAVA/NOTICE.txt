Thu muc JAVA/ nay khong phai do RetroHub viet. No dong goi lai phan mem cua
nguoi khac de chay game J2ME tren may cam tay. Duoi day la tung thanh phan,
giay phep, va cho lay ma nguon.

This JAVA/ directory is not written by RetroHub. It repackages other people's
software so that J2ME games run on the handheld. Each component, its licence
and where to get its source is listed below.

Day KHONG phai ban goc cua thuong nguon. Day la mot ban phan nhanh co sua doi

This is NOT the upstream build. It is a modified fork for the TrimUI Brick Pro,
compiled 2025-08-21, adding: renderer.conf (three display modes pixel/smooth/hq),
the H/K/X pad-layout cycle stored in control_profile.cfg and control_cycle.cfg,
and an exit confirmation dialog.

RetroHub CON SUA THEM vao ban .jar nay, o muc bytecode, bon cho:

  1. Display$SerialCallTimerTask.run
     removeElement(Integer.valueOf(0))  ->  removeElementAt(0)
  2. Displayable.notifySetCurrent   rong  ->  goi render()
  3. Displayable.keyPressed(int)    rong  ->  goi keyPressedCommands(int)
  4. TextBox.<init> va TextBox.setString  ->  luon cat mot chuoi mac dinh

Ba cho dau sua loi khien man hinh khong phai Canvas khong bao gio duoc ve va
khong nhan duoc phim. Cho thu tu la mot cach lam viec quanh: may cam tay khong
co ban phim va ban gia lap nay khong co ban phim ao.

Cong cu tai lap nam trong ma nguon RetroHub tai tools/patch_j2me_runtime.py.
Chay no tren ban .jar goc se ra dung ban .jar di kem day. Tep do ghi ro tung
byte bi doi va ly do - day la thu thay cho ma nguon ma chung toi co the cung
cap trung thuc, vi ma nguon cua ban phan nhanh khong nam trong tay chung toi.

RetroHub ALSO MODIFIES this .jar at the bytecode level, in four places - see
the list above. The first three fix screens other than Canvas never being drawn
and never receiving keys; the fourth works around the handheld having no
keyboard and this build having no on-screen one.

The tool that reproduces them is tools/patch_j2me_runtime.py in the RetroHub
source. Running it against the upstream .jar produces exactly the .jar shipped
here. It documents every changed byte and why, which is the honest stand-in we
can offer for source we do not have.
