RetroHub - thanh phan cua ben thu ba / third-party components
==============================================================================

RetroHub dong goi lai mot so phan mem cua nguoi khac. Duoi day la tung thanh
phan, giay phep, va cho lay ban goc.

RetroHub bundles software written by other people. Each component, its licence
and where the original came from is listed below.

Ban chay J2ME (thu muc Emus/JAVA, chi co trong ban cai day du) co danh sach
rieng trong JAVA/NOTICE.txt.
The J2ME runtime (Emus/JAVA, full package only) has its own list in
JAVA/NOTICE.txt.

Giay phep / Licence : Python Software Foundation License 2.0
Toan van / Full text : python/lib/python3.11/LICENSE.txt

                      Public Domain

Thuong nguon / Upstream : https://github.com/py-sdl/py-sdl2

CO MOT SUA DOI: vendor/sdl2/dll.py khong phai ban goc. No mang ban va cua

ONE MODIFICATION: vendor/sdl2/dll.py is not stock. It carries PortMaster's

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SDL2, SDL2_ttf, SDL2_image  ->  KHONG di kem / NOT bundled
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RetroHub dung thu vien SDL2 co san cua may tai /usr/trimui/lib. Chung thuoc ve
nha san xuat may, khong duoc phan phat kem RetroHub.
RetroHub uses the device's own SDL2 libraries at /usr/trimui/lib. They belong to
the device vendor and are not redistributed with RetroHub.

Kho game / Game catalogue
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RetroHub khong luu tru tep game nao. Danh muc chi chua duong dan toi cac nguon
san co tren Internet, do ben thu ba van hanh. Moi tua game thuoc ban quyen cua
chu so huu goc.

RetroHub hosts no game files. The catalogue holds links to third-party sources
already on the Internet. Every title remains the copyright of its owner.
