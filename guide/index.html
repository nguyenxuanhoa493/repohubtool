Format SD Card as exFAT

Use a reliable MicroSD card (SanDisk, Samsung genuine 64GB – 256GB). Insert into your computer via an SD card reader.

MANDATORY: FORMAT AS exFAT — DO NOT USE FAT32:
FAT32 cannot store files larger than 4GB, lacks compatibility with modern packages, and frequently causes boot freezes on TrimUI hardware.
  1. On Windows:
    • Open This PC → Right-click your SD card drive → Select Format...
    • Under File system, choose exFAT (Allocation unit size: Default).
    • Check Quick Format → Click Start and wait a few seconds.
  2. On macOS:
    • Open the Disk Utility application.
    • Select the SD card on the left sidebar → Click Erase on the top toolbar.
    • Under Format, select ExFAT (Scheme: Master Boot Record) → Click Erase.

Extract & Copy All Folders to SD Card

  3. Select all folders inside and click Copy.
  4. Open your formatted SD card root and click Paste directly into the top level of the card.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Troubleshooting & Tips

  • My device is stuck at the TrimUI logo on boot?

    This is almost always caused by formatting as FAT32 or faulty partitions. Re-format your card as exFAT (Step 2) and copy the files again.

  • Does this Full package include games already?

    It comes with the complete system base, BIOS, emulators, and RetroHub. Connect to Wi-Fi and open RetroHub to download from the 40,000 game library directly on device, or transfer ROMs from PC via SFTP.

  • Where do I copy ROMs from my PC?

    Place them into /Roms/[CONSOLE]/ on your SD card (e.g. GBA games into /Roms/GBA/). On your TrimUI home screen, press Menu and select Refresh Roms.

  • How do updates work in the future?

    100% automated! Connect your console to Wi-Fi and open RetroHub. It automatically detects and installs updates on-device in seconds.
