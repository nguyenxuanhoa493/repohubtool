Superfast 3-Step Setup for Beginners

Just got your TrimUI handheld or starting with a blank SD card? No need to download multiple separate packages. The complete system base, verified emulators, and RetroHub are bundled into one single ZIP:

STEP 1

1. Download All-in-One Package

Download a single comprehensive ZIP (1.05 GB) containing: Stock OS base, full BIOS, RetroArch cores, Java J2ME, Sega CD, and latest RetroHub v1.97.

Download All-in-One (1.05 GB) ⤓
Pre-configured: tg4040 SD base, Java J2ME runtime, 40,000 game catalog, direct GitHub Release download.
STEP 2

2. Format SD Card as exFAT

Prepare a genuine MicroSD card (64GB – 256GB). Insert into your PC and format to exFAT:
• Windows: Right-click SD drive → Format... → File system: exFAT → Click Start.
• macOS: Open Disk Utility → Select SD → Erase → Format: ExFAT.

Mandatory exFAT: Never use FAT32 to avoid file size limits and TrimUI logo boot freeze.
STEP 3

3. Copy to SD Card & Done!

Extract the downloaded ZIP using 7-Zip or WinRAR. Copy all internal folders (Apps, Emus, RetroArch, Roms, System,...) directly to the root of your SD card.

Done & Ready: Insert SD card into TrimUI, power on, and play! Open Apps → RetroHub to fetch games over Wi-Fi.
Wireless Automation: You only need a computer once. Future updates and new game downloads are handled 100% wirelessly over Wi-Fi right on the console!

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Troubleshooting & Tips

  • My device is stuck at the TrimUI logo on boot?

    This is almost always caused by formatting as FAT32 or faulty partitions. Re-format your card as exFAT (Step 2) and copy the files again.

  • Does this Full package include games already?

    It comes with the complete system base, BIOS, emulators, and RetroHub. Connect to Wi-Fi and open RetroHub to download from the 40,000 game library directly on device, or transfer ROMs from PC via SFTP.

  • Where do I copy ROMs from my PC?

    Place them into /Roms/[CONSOLE]/ on your SD card (e.g. GBA games into /Roms/GBA/). On your TrimUI home screen, press Menu and select Refresh Roms.

  • How do updates work in the future?

    100% automated! Connect your console to Wi-Fi and open RetroHub. It automatically detects and installs updates on-device in seconds.
