7-Step Setup Guide for TrimUI Brick Pro

Just got your TrimUI Brick Pro handheld or starting with a fresh SD card? Follow these 7 straightforward steps to install full emulators and RetroHub:

1

1. Download Stock Full ROMs

Get the full emulator base from the official DTH-RetroHandheld release page.

Open DTH Releases ↗
2

2. Format SD Card as exFAT

Insert your MicroSD card (64GB - 256GB) into PC and format to exFAT (mandatory to avoid card recognition errors).

3

3. Extract ROMs to SD Card

Extract the downloaded archive using 7-Zip or WinRAR. Copy all internal folders (Apps, Emus, Roms, System...) directly to SD card root.

4

4. Reboot Console to Load ROMs

Insert the SD card into TrimUI and power on once so the OS registers and initializes all emulators.

5

5. Download Latest RetroHub

Download the latest RetroHub v2.33 installation package for Stock OS:

Download RetroHub (Stock) v2.33 ⤓
6

6. Copy RetroHub to Apps

Extract the file from Step 5, copy the RetroHub folder into /Apps/ on your SD card.

7

7. Reboot & Open App

Turn on your console, navigate to Apps → RetroHub to access 40,000+ games and enjoy Wi-Fi auto-updates!

Wireless Automation: You only perform these 7 steps via computer once. All future updates are handled wirelessly over Wi-Fi right on the console!

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Troubleshooting & Tips

  • My device is stuck at the TrimUI logo on boot?

    This is almost always caused by formatting as FAT32. Re-format your card as exFAT (Step 2) and copy the files again.

  • Where do I copy ROMs from my PC?

    Place them into /Roms/[CONSOLE]/ on your SD card (e.g. GBA games into /Roms/GBA/). On your TrimUI home screen, press Menu → Refresh Roms.

  • How do updates work in the future?

    100% automated! Connect your console to Wi-Fi and open RetroHub. It automatically detects and installs updates on-device in seconds.
