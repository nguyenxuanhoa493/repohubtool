    How to install & update

    1. For TrimUI (Stock OS / CrossMix): Unpack RetroHub-1.63-full.zip and copy the RetroHub folder into /Apps/ on your SD card. Open Apps → RetroHub.
    2. For NextUI: Unpack RetroHub-1.63-NextUI.zip and copy the Tools folder directly to your SD card root (lands in /Tools/tg5040/RetroHub.pak/). Open Tools → RetroHub.
    3. Online updates: Connect your handheld to Wi-Fi. RetroHub checks for updates automatically every time it opens and downloads only what changed.

    You only install manually once. From then on, the app updates automatically over Wi-Fi on both TrimUI Stock OS and NextUI.
